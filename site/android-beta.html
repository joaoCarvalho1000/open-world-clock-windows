Try the Android™ beta

Two quick steps. Use the same Google account for both.

  1. 1. Join the group

    Choose “Join group” on Google Groups.

    Join Google Group

  2. 2. Install the app

    Choose “Become a tester”, then download from Google Play.

Try it for 14 days and send feedback.

Need help?

No access? Join the group first, check your Google account, then try the Play link again in a few minutes. If you are in the internal test, leave it first.

You need a compatible Android device and an eligible Google account. Country, supervised-child and work-account restrictions may apply.

This is an early build. Purchases are not automatically free for testers.

To leave, choose Leave the program on the Play test page. You can also leave the group.
